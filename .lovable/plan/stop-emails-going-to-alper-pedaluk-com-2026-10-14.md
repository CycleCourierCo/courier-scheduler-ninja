# Stop emails going to alper@pedaluk.com

## What I found
- **"Fill in my details" is not the cause.** It uses the profile email, which is already info@trybeforeyoubike.com.
- **The saved address book contact is.** His address book still has a "Alper Muduroglu" contact with alper@pedaluk.com (last used 12 Oct). Picking that contact puts the old address on the job.
- **3 open jobs** have him as sender and **1** as receiver with the old address. Each job keeps its own copy of the contact details, so emails for those jobs still go to alper@pedaluk.com.
- **His sign-in email is still alper@pedaluk.com.** Only the profile was changed, not the login. Password-reset and sign-in emails will still go to the old address.
- The accounts email stays as alper@pedaluk.com, as you asked.

## The fix
1. Change the saved address book contact to info@trybeforeyoubike.com.
2. Change the sender/receiver email on the 4 open jobs (only jobs that aren't delivered or cancelled). Finished jobs stay as they are.
3. Change his sign-in email to info@trybeforeyoubike.com (the same as User Management → Edit User). His password stays the same.

This is a one-off data fix. No change to how the app works.
